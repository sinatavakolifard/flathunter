About this tool

A private apartment-search bot. It checks the major German property portals every few minutes, keeps the listings that match your criteria, and sends them to you before you would have found them by hand.

What it watches

  • ImmoScout24 — via its mobile API, so no login and no captcha
  • Immowelt
  • Kleinanzeigen
  • WG-Gesucht

Why bother

In a tight rental market a listing can collect hundreds of applications within an hour of going up. That rewards whoever happens to be refreshing the page at the right moment. Checking every portal on your behalf, every ten minutes, removes the need to be lucky with timing.

How it works

The crawler runs with ./run.sh. Each pass fetches the search URLs listed in config.yaml, drops anything outside your price and size range, skips listings it has already reported, and notifies you about the rest. Every listing it has seen is stored in a local SQLite file and shown on the Listings page.

No accounts, no analytics, no third-party scripts. Nothing logs in to any portal on your behalf, and these pages load no external scripts, fonts or trackers. One caveat worth knowing: listing thumbnails are loaded directly from the portals' image servers, so opening this page does reveal your IP address to them — the same as visiting their sites would.

Adjusting the search

Everything is configured in config.yaml: the search URLs, the filters: block for price, size and rooms, excluded title patterns, and how often it polls. Restart the crawler after editing it.
